Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to run your Korean classes and payments, we keep it in the EU (Ireland), we never sell it, and you can ask to see or delete it at any time by emailing hazelminjeong@gmail.com.

Who we are

Hangeul Institute Dublin (한글학당) is run by Minjeong Kang, a registered sole trader, Jervis St, North City, Dublin 1, D01 KV56, Ireland. She is responsible ("data controller") for your personal data in this app.

Contact for anything about your data: hazelminjeong@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored encrypted; nobody, including the teacher, can read it.
  • Class details: your class or trial, private lesson bookings, attendance and your payment reference (for example HI-1002).
  • Payment records: what you owe, what you paid and when. We do not collect card or bank details: you pay by bank transfer from your own bank.
  • Your photo choice: whether you agree to class photos on Instagram.
  • Game Day guests: name and a phone number or email, entered by the teacher when you book.
  • Technical data: the services below may record your IP address and browser type for security, as most websites do.

Why we use it, and the legal basis

What forLegal basis (GDPR)
Running your classes: schedule, attendance, private lessons, homework and online class accessContract: we need it to provide the classes you signed up for
Payments and receipts, and keeping financial recordsContract and legal obligation (tax records)
Sending account emails (confirm your email, reset your password)Contract
Posting class photos of you on InstagramYour consent: only if you tick the box, and you can untick it any time
Keeping the app secureLegitimate interest

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use tracking or analytics cookies.

Who else handles your data

We use a few trusted services to run the app. They only process your data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: stores the app's data and logins, on servers in Ireland (EU).
  • Google (Gmail): sends the app's account emails from our Gmail address.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's web pages.
  • Google Fonts: provides the app's fonts.
  • Google Classroom (homework) and Microsoft Teams (online classes): when you use these, you use your own account with them and their own privacy policies apply.

Some of these companies are based outside the EU. Where your data leaves the EU, they use the safeguards GDPR requires (such as the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long we keep it

  • Account and class details: while you are a student, and deleted within 12 months after your last class, or sooner if you ask.
  • Payment records: 6 years, because Irish tax law requires us to keep financial records.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see a copy of your data,
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • delete your data (except payment records we must keep for tax),
  • restrict or object to how we use it, or receive it in a portable format,
  • withdraw your photo consent (untick the box in the app's Info tab).

Email hazelminjeong@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Storage on your device

The app saves a small login token in your browser so you stay logged in. This is strictly necessary for the app to work, so it does not need a cookie banner. Logging out removes it.

Age

Our classes are for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top and tell students in the app.
